## Who we are

**Alayra Systems** is an engineering company with teams in **Pakistan** and the **United States**. We build developer tools and AI infrastructure for people and organizations that want the power of modern AI models **without handing their code, keys, or telemetry to someone else's cloud**.

Two products, one belief: **you should own your AI stack.**

### Alayra Nexus
**The open-source enterprise AI gateway.**

Unify Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral, HuggingFace, Cloudflare Workers AI, or anything OpenAI-compatible behind a single endpoint.

- Bring your own provider keys, pool and load-balance them
- Automatic failover between tiers
- Scoped team keys, budgets, and rate limits
- Usage analytics and cost tracking
- Backup and restore, clustering, audit trail
- Self-host in minutes, or use Nexus Cloud

[**Open the repo**](https://github.com/Alayra-Systems-Pvt-Limited/Alayra-Nexus) · [Live demo](https://alayra-systems-pvt-limited.github.io/Alayra-Nexus/demo/) · [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@alayrasystems/nexus)

### Kinetic IDE
**An agentic IDE with intelligence without surveillance.**

A security-hardened development environment where AI reasoning, tool execution, and indexing stay under your control.

- Real-time telemetry of what the agent is doing

## What we care about

- **Sovereignty.** Your keys, your code, your data boundary.
- **Production-grade from day one.** Routing, failover, backups, audit, and security are features, not roadmap items.
- **Honest engineering.** We publish what is shipped and label what is not.
- **Open where it counts.** Nexus is open source so you can read it, run it, and trust it.
